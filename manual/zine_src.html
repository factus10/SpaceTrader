01Loading the program

One TAP file of about 110K: the BASIC program, machine code, data, a font, the title screen and twenty system pictures.

  1. Set the machine to TS2068 or TC2068.
  2. Open spacetrader.tap.
  3. Type LOAD "" and press ENTER.

It starts itself. Press a key at the title, then name your ship. ENTER alone names her Beowulf.

Leave fast loading onThe program pulls blocks off the tape as it runs, including a picture for every system you visit.
Don't use RUNIt starts without reserving the memory the program needs. Let it start itself, or GO TO 40.

02The screen

The top third is the world you are docked at; it changes when you jump. Below it the market, headed with the system's name, tech level and law level — the two numbers that decide what things cost and how much trouble you are in.

MarkMeansPrice
EXPort exports it−30%
IMPort imports it+40%
--Neither—

An exclamation mark means that good is contraband here. The bottom line is always credits, fuel, hull and heat.

Menu keys show in yellow — press that letter. A message takes the menu's place and shows in reverse; any key clears it.

03Trading

Buy where a good is exported, sell where it is imported. That is the whole engine.

Population and tech level shift the price too, and on top of that a swing of up to 15% either way, rolled afresh every time you dock. A bad price is worth coming back for. The hold takes twenty tons.

The richest single jump is luxuries from Ambergris into Junction Nine, about 3,400 credits on a full hold. A typical good run pays 1,200.

04The sector

Twenty systems. You can jump within two columns and two rows, so two squares diagonally is still one jump. Fuel is one ton plus one per two squares — one to three a jump, from a tank of eight.

Core to frontier runs left to right. The core is cheap fuel, cheap repairs and inspectors everywhere. The frontier is the reverse: nothing legal is worth much, nothing is illegal, and the pirates know it.

Ossuary and Vagrant are six jumps out, and Ossuary connects only through Kalideen.

05Contraband and licenses

Four goods are restricted. A good is contraband anywhere whose law level is above its threshold.

GoodAbove lawIllegal inLicense
Weapons49/201,440
Luxuries56/202,000
Electronics63/201,120
Medical72/20720

Buying contraband adds 2 heat. A license, bought at any port, stops that good being contraband for you anywhere — the exclamation mark goes and customs stop caring.

Which firstWeapons is illegal in nine systems and costs 1,440. One core inspection costs more than the license.

06Heat

Heat is official attention, and it makes three things worse at once: customs inspect you more often, pirates find you more often, and bribes stop working.

What you doHeat
Buy a ton of contraband+2
Surrender cargo+1
Pay a fine+4
Bribe refused+8
Resist inspection+15
Every jump−1

There is no way to pay heat off. The only cure is time: jump, trade clean, let it fall. A run of quiet jumps is worth making before a smuggling trip, not after.

07Customs

You are inspected on arrival, and sometimes stopped in transit. The chance is the world's law level plus a fifth of your heat, never above 70%. Transit stops are half as likely.

Surrender when caught cold — you lose the goods anyway, and it is the fine and the heat that hurt.

08Pirates and patrols

Every jump rolls against the law level of where you are going. Heat is added to both chances.

10The mortgage, and the end

You start owing 20,000 credits. Every eight jumps 800 falls due and is taken automatically if you have it. The port screen shows the balance and the jumps remaining.

Miss one and you are warned. Miss two and the ship is repossessed.

The minimum alone takes two hundred jumps. Paying down the principal with M after a good run clears it in a handful. The minimum keeps you flying; the profits buy you out.

Keep 800 in handGoing into the eighth jump broke is how most ships are lost — not to pirates, but to the bank.

Winning and losing

Clear the debt and the ship is yours. That is the only way to win.

Three ways to lose

  • Hull reaches zero in a fight.
  • Two missed payments and the bank takes her.
  • Stranded — no fuel, nothing to sell, and not enough credits for a single ton.

The third is the quiet one. Nothing warns you.

11Tips

  • Run the core first. Electronics and machinery out of Coriolis Gate, Verity and Tannhauser pay well and legally, and the core is almost free of pirates.
  • Buy the weapons license before the weapons. One core inspection costs more than the license did.
  • Watch the market, not the good. Prices reroll every docking; a 15% swing can beat choosing a different cargo.
  • Fill the tank before a frontier run. Fuel is cheapest in the core, it is your escape chance, and there is none out there.
  • Let heat fall before the big trip. It is added to the pirate chance and the inspection chance and taken off your bribe odds.
  • Surrender rather than bribe when hot. At 30 heat a bribe is 40% nothing against 60% double fine and 8 more heat.
  • Pay the principal when you can. A thousand off the debt beats a thousand in the account, because the account is what fines and pirates take.
